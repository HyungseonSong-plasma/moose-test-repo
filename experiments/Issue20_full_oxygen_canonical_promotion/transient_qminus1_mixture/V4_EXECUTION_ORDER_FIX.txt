R14 EVR1-D v4 execution-order correction

Observed v3:
  PREPARE PASS
  CHECK-INPUT PASS
  SOLVE PASS
  CHECK FAIL

The v3 values exposed an exact one-timestep lag:

  FD Sdot at 0.0002  = -1.284108539962858
  model Sdot at 0.0003 = -1.284108542196800

  FD Sdot at 0.0003  = -1.241803919995732
  model Sdot at 0.0004 = -1.241803914467700

  FD Sdot at 0.0004  = -1.201261960019905
  model Sdot at 0.0005 = -1.201261965757300

Root cause:
  TimeDerivativeAux(dw_k/dt) and FunctorAux(derived derivative) were both
  scheduled at TIMESTEP_END. The derived FunctorAux could read the previous
  dw_k/dt Aux state because same-stage execution order was not guaranteed.

v4:
  - keep direct TimeDerivativeAux(dw_k) unchanged;
  - remove derived FunctorAux copies for invMn_dot, dMn_dt, drho_dt;
  - evaluate invMn_dot_model, dMn_dt_model, drho_dt_model directly with
    ElementAverageFunctorPostprocessor after the AuxKernel stage;
  - independently reconstruct Sdot in the checker from current direct
    dw_O/dt, dw_Om/dt, dw_Op/dt, dw_Os/dt averages;
  - retain exact S=1/Mn BE finite-difference parity.

No physics, transport coefficient, timestep, mesh, solver tolerance, or
production source changed.
