## HPEM vs LXCat access: current authoritative statements

- **HPEM:** https://cpseg.eecs.umich.edu/data.html hosts the public *reaction list* `e_reactions.pdf`, but directs researchers to contact M. Kushner for original **numerical energy-dependent electron cross sections**, specifying collision IDs, energy window and resolution. Thus publicly listed IDs and thresholds do **not** imply an available downloadable numeric table.
- **LXCat:** https://master.lxcat.net/contributors/ is an open-access scattering database. Its Phelps subset documents 18 O2 records; IST-Lisbon contains 15 O2 records; IAA has e+O (18) and e+O2 (69); Morgan includes O, O2 and O- related records. These are *database-level inventory counts*, not proof of a specific HPEM equivalent channel.
- **Terms:** https://us.lxcat.net/instructions/redistribution.php says downloaders should acknowledge terms and credit original contributors; **do not redistribute original numerical tables in a public repository without authorisation**.
- **LXCat 3:** https://future.lxcat.net/developer says the demo API requires authentication and its datasets are not stable for publications; use published LXCat 2.

## Important numeric-domain and chemical-mapping restrictions

1. **EI-411 (4.5 eV):** The demo file includes 13 source energy knots only up to **15 eV**; this is **not** complete coverage to 200 eV. The Phelps label `O2(4.5eV)` is an electronic-energy-loss proxy, **not a verified direct 2O(3P) chemical product**.
2. **KIN-V01:** The demo low-energy v1 curve ends at **3.5 eV**; its separately mapped 9 eV resonance begins at **4 eV**. A validated interpolation or source reconstruction policy is required for the gap.
3. **EI-412:** The demo `O2(6.0eV)` energy-loss curve is not the same reaction description as separately compiled Cosby dissociation; adding both as independent full energy sinks is prohibited without reconciliation.
4. **EI-413/414:** Their demo proxy products are `O2(8.4eV)` and `O2(9.97eV)`. Do not silently map them to O2(a1Delta) metastable population.
5. **EI-401:** Cantera's published electron attachment process uses `O^-+O`, but its threshold field is 0.0 and first nonzero energy knot is 4.4 eV. HPEM identifies #401 at 4.9 eV. Preserve the mismatch; do not equate threshold conventions.
6. **KIN-M01:** Phelps O2 `EFFECTIVE` Qm is already selected for O2 momentum transfer; it does not replace independent elastic recoil-energy information. Atomic O elastic `KIN-EL-O` remains separate.
7. **Unmatched Chemistry:** EI-417 (O+ *partial* ionization), EI-2259 (e+O2+ dissociative recombination), EI-2058/2067/2068/2069/2072/2073/2074 (excited O2 target), EI-943/944 (excited atomic-O target), EI-935/936/941 (atomic-O electronic channels), EI-2738 (e+O- detachment) remain **process-level numerical UNVERIFIED** in this review. IAA/Morgan/IST/TRINITI may supply candidate O and O- curves, but target products, charge, electronic state, and differential/total conventions must match. H23–H25 are QDB heavy formulas, not electron cross sections.

## How to acquire the numerical data locally without redistributing it

1. For authoritative source, browse https://master.lxcat.net/data/set_type.php , select *Scattering Cross Sections*, then Phelps or alternative IST-Lisbon/IAA/Morgan and the exact species/processes. Accept the site's data terms and download the native LXCat/BOLSIG+ file to a **private local path**; record retrieval time, file checksum, source database/reference and valid units. Keep out of public GitHub.
2. As an **intermediate numeric demonstration only**, obtain the unchanged public [Cantera/Phelps YAML](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera-example-data/blob/main/air-plasma-Phelps.yaml) from its upstream owner and run:
   
   ```bash
   CHEM=experiments/Issue395_powered_chemistry_ladder
   python3 "$CHEM/acquire_lxcat_cantera_o2_candidates.py" \
     --source /local/private/air-plasma-Phelps.yaml \
     --output-dir "$CHEM/.local_cross_sections/cantera_o2_candidates" \
     --retrieved-on 2026-10-30
   ```

   This local-only extractor requires exactly 14 process-matched numerical source blocks and writes **14 CSV candidate vectors plus a SHA-256 manifest**. It refuses to select unapproved chemistry and sets `solver_accepted=false` and `hpem_equivalence=false` for every vector. **These local files have NOT been generated on this executor because the original source bytes could be fetched through GitHub's data tool but not downloaded into a writable filesystem**; do not claim that local numerical acquisition is complete.
3. After obtaining original LXCat data, verify original process text/threshold/reference (not just count), compare to the Cantera demonstration vectors, resolve source energy intervals, source permissions, mass/recoil/superelastic conventions and solve Boltzmann against independent BOLSIG+ evidence. Only then change numeric status to `VERIFIED`.
