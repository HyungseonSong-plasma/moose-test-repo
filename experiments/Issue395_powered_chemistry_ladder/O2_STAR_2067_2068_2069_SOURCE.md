## Source and scientific distinction

1. **HPEM listing** explicitly assigns ICOLION=0 to all three channels. The 2067 HPEM number `-0.98` is a *negative electron energy loss*: electron **gains** about 0.977 eV, while 2068/2069 remove 3.52/5.02 eV in the HPEM model. HPEM numbers 4.5/6.0 eV occur in comments referring to the parent O2(X) channels; do not put these in the excited-target energy-loss column.
2. **407 reverse**: forward `e+O2(X)->e+O2(a)` has `Delta=0.977 eV`, ground/a degeneracy ratio `g_X/g_a=3/2`. Inverse (electron collision energy epsilon >= 0):

   `sigma_2067(epsilon) = (3/2)*((epsilon+Delta)/epsilon)*sigma_407(epsilon+Delta)` for epsilon > 0.

   At epsilon=0 treat through an explicit low-energy limiting/interpolation model; never divide by zero. Do not treat inverse sigma as an independent measured Phelps channel. Use verified 407 source and record hashes; do not publish protected original arrays.
3. **2067 rate benchmark**: Sungjin Kim (2006), Appendix A, derives `k_2067(Te) = (3/2)*exp(Delta/Te)*k_407(Te) = 2.055e-15 exp(-1.163/Te)` m3/s, commonly rounded `2.06e-15`. This identity assumes a Maxwellian electron distribution and valid common Te. The primary future rate must instead integrate reverse sigma against the actual self-consistent EEDF; this benchmark is not independent of 407's fit.
4. **2068 physical gate**: Ground-state O2 dissociation into two O(3P) has minimum about 5.12 eV; starting from O2(a1Delta_g), the minimum is about 5.12-0.977=4.14 eV. HPEM's 3.52 eV channel value is below that minimum. Moreover, original Phelps `O2 4.5 LOSS` is an electronic energy-loss channel, not proof of O(3P)+O(3P) production. Do not create a chemistry O source or rate lookup for 2068 before the reaction identity and energy bookkeeping are scientifically repaired. This is the same hazard as registry 411.
5. **2069 parent identity gate**: HPEM `O2*-ELECTRONIC 6.0eV` likely corresponds to an energy-shift model from the ground-state 412/6 eV family; source-listing metadata alone do not establish a measured excited-target cross section. Phelps `O2 6.0 LOSS` energy-loss cross section and its independent `O2 DISSOCIATION` (Cosby, 1993) cross section are not interchangeable. A literature review describes excited-state dissociation via a threshold shift of *corresponding ground-state dissociation* sigma; its validity and branching are a model assumption requiring separate acceptance. The 5.02 eV HPEM value is above the 4.14 eV thermodynamic minimum but is not therefore proof of correct kinetics.
6. **2068/2069 double counting**: They share reactants/products. Treat as alternative competing effective kinetic channels unless independent, non-overlapping physical branches are established. Do not sum both 4.5-loss and 6.0-loss curves with O-atom yields of two without a documented partition/branching audit. Different energy-loss labels are not sufficient evidence of independent production paths.
7. **Source request**: Request HPEM `RFXSECS_NEW.F` reaction-special-numbers 2068 and 2069 individually (and 2067 if an independently stored inverse exists), with electron-energy grid/units, target electronic/vibrational state, parent data, threshold shift or other transformation, branching/products, and redistribution rights. See https://cpseg.eecs.umich.edu/data.html .
